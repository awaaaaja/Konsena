1. Microphone access
The SuaraRapat application requests the Android microphone permission. The app uses the microphone when you tap Start Recording and stops capturing audio when you tap Stop.

2. File retention
Recordings stay on the device until you delete them or choose Export. You can review the destination before an export begins.

3. User controls
You can use the written notes feature if you decline the microphone permission. A recording indicator stays visible while a recording is running.
